# Instrimate

## What is it?

A useful tool for teachers and professors to manage their classes and track programs
and keep up with important events and plans and meet deadlines.

## Why should I use it as a teacher or professor?

You don't need to, but if you find yourself lacking time and losing track of plans
end not meeting the deadlines, so this tool can help you.
I am a part time English teacher at a local educational center. As I was teaching
and working as a programmer, I constantly lost track of plans, deadlines, timeline
for courses and classes and tracking progress of my students. As a result, I built
this to help me live better.

## What services do we provide?

As a teacher, I don't personally like apps that provides tons of services that
neither help us to focus on our work nor get revenue back from those unused
features. So, I am making this with minimum needed features.
Our platform serve these services:

- Teaching objectives: For each of your classes. we ask you in any interval as your
  choice daily or weekly about what needs to be taught at your classes. And help you
  plan around it. Set due dates to keep up with time and life.

- Assignments: For each of your class we ask you about the assignments, and their
  due dates.

- Reports: we help you to create precise reports for your classes daily, weekly, and
  by end of each course.

- Plans & events: We let you to set your important events and plans on the calendar
  to not forget them. We remind you when they need to be done or be are heppeing in a
  time of your choice before their occurrence.

If you care, we have the following tech stack.
We use NestJS for better scalability and its modular architecture. We use PostgreSQL
for the database and prisma ORM. We use React for the web app and React Native or Axpo.
