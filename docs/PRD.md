# Instrimate

## What is it?

Instrimate is a platfrom to help teachers teach conveniently by assisting teachers
with their plans, roadmaps, and deadlines. We also improve the connection between
teachers and students -or parents, with group chats and and student's profiles and
leader boards to create competetion between individuals.

## Why we build it?

The reason I have decided to build it, was to help myself. I am a part-time English
at a local educational center. As the number of classes and students grow, it gets
really tedious to keep up with all of the classes and students. I frequently lose
track of individuals and classes as a whole. like what was class X homework, what
I must teach to class Y and lessons and parts remained form previous session. When
students strugled to keep up with lessons and assignments, it gets really hard to
contact individual's supervisors/parents to talk and make an effort to find the
cause and suggest solutions. Another task that can become very tedious sometimes is
the reports. When the manager/parents request for a report, it is just neither very
convenient nor enough to create reports out of everyday log or worsely just from
memeory. It also have a very negative impression and even trust issues on our skills
and us. So, here I came up with an idea to make a platform to help teachers provide
better and convenient educations.

## What we are not?

You might think that we are just another online platforms like Preply and Coursera,
while we really admire their work and their services we are solving other problems.
here is a list of services that we don't provide:

- We don't provide a rea one-to-one learning.
- we don't provide online classes through video calls or other sorts.

## What services do we provide?

As a teacher, I don't personally like apps that provides tons of services that
neither help us to focus on our work nor get revenue back from those unused
features. So, I am making this minmal and focussed to make a place like home devoid
of any noise. Here is a list of our services that our platform provide:

- **Teaching Space**: For each teacher we provide a teaching space which within he/she
  can see their classes, students, meetings, roadmaps, and events in a clean UI.
- **classes within Spaces**: For each real class/course, teachers can create the equivelent
  classes there, too. Each class has:
  - **Roadmaps**: The teaching plan over time, like what is going to be tuaght by what date.
  - **Exam Dates**: Weekly, mid-term, final exams, with their dates.
  - **Student's profile**: Each student get their own profile that contains individuals
    contact information, progress, achievements, attendance, registeration info.
    Just to Note, that contact information is only visible to teacher.
  - **Chat**: Each class get's it's own group chat for connection and solving each other's
    issues and colaborating.
  - **Attendance**: Each class gets it's own attendance with can be converted into document
    or tabular sheets with dynamic ranges weekly/monthly/quarterly.
  - **Assignments**: Daily, weekly, sessional, lessonal assignments.
  - **Reports**: The most favorite one. For each class you can create reports daily, weekly,
    and monthly which become downloadable files in xls for docx or txt formats. We set list
    of useful questions that can be asked you to log each days/sessions/lessons progress.
    You can easily update them and/or add you own to help you log the progress and performance
    of each students or the whole class.
  - **Resources**: Teachers can upload main or extra curriculan resouces to make at easy to
    see and access and plan on.

- **Offline usage**: we provided in device storages to make it possible to use the app when
  offline and automatilly sync with cloud once connected to internet, to make super convenient
  to use in mutiple devices simultanously.
